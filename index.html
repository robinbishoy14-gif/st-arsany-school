Who We Are

At St. Arsany School, we are proud to welcome our students and families to a caring, inclusive and inspiring educational community.

From Kindergarten through Secondary, our students follow the Egyptian national curriculum alongside dedicated foreign-language study, in classrooms that value discipline, curiosity and care in equal measure.
